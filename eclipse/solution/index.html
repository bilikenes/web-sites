Satellites

Built to be light, to pack tightly into a launch vehicle, and to operate reliably in orbit for years.

Ground network

HOW IT WORKS

A network that talks to itself.

Satellites connect with devices, ground stations, and each other, enabling data to travel via the fastest route without depending on one ground connection.

Satellite to Satellite

Inter-satellite links relay data cross the constellation in orbit. Extending reach, increasing resilience, reducing latency.

Satellite to Ground

Direct connectivity between orbit and devices, terminals, or ground stations on the surface.

HOW WE DELIVER

We work with you from specification to handoff.

The engagement is one continuous program, not a chain of contracts. We work through it together, in four stages.

01

Consultation

We start with the mission requirements: coverage, capacity, frequency, latency, security posture, sovereignty constraints.

02

Design

We design the constellation against those requirements end-to-end, satellites and payload together, no handoffs between vendors.

03

Delivery

We deliver through milestone-structured manufacturing over an 18–36 month window.

04

Handover

The customer takes operational control of the constellation, while we provide ongoing operations support under a separate contract.
